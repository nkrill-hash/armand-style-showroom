Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you cut boards to size?

Yes. We cut and edge melamine, super wood and granite tops to your sizes. Send your measurements on WhatsApp or through the quote form and we will confirm price and availability.

How quickly will I get a quote?

We confirm stock, pricing and delivery within 2 hours on trading days. Message us on WhatsApp for the fastest reply.

How can I pay?

We accept EcoCash, Paynow, EFT and bank transfer.

Do you deliver?

Yes, nationwide delivery is available. Tell us where you are and we will confirm the delivery options for your area.

Do you offer trade accounts?

Yes. Builders, joiners, designers and contractors can open a trade account for preferential pricing. See our Trade page to apply.

Where are you and when are you open?

We are at 2142 Khami Rd, Iversens Complex, Bulawayo. Open Monday to Friday 08:00 to 17:00 and Saturday 08:00 to 13:00.

Ready to Order?

WhatsApp us your product list or spec sheet — we'll confirm stock, pricing and delivery within 2 hours.
